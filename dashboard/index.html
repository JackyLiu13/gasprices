Log a price / rebuild

Logging one price is what calibrates a station's offset — it is the highest-value thing you can do here. Rebuild reruns backend/build.py and rewrites your working tree; committing stays manual.

Retail margin

    
Each dot is one observed price inverted through the tax stack.
      The amber line is the 90-day rolling median — the number
      calibrate_margin() would actually have used that day.

    

    

  


  

    
Forecast accuracy

    
MAE per horizon against the only baseline that matters: assuming
      no move at all. A bar below the grey line is the model losing to
      doing nothing.

Station ladder

    
Offset is median(observed − benchmark); MAD is its spread.
      Where beats when by about 5×, so this table is the main axis — the timing
      verdicts are the minor one.
